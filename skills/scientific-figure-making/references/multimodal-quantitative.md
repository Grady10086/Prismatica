# Quantitative multimodal and diffusion plates

Use `examples/multimodal_diffusion_plate.py --out PATH` for three compact original layouts. Dependencies: numpy, matplotlib and scipy. It exports PNG/SVG/PDF, all source arrays in `multimodal-diffusion-inputs.npz`, and provenance. These are data/composition fixtures, not a CSV importer or pretrained model benchmark. Gallery masters are 11.5 × 6.5–7 inches; adapt panel count and typography to the final venue size.

- **Retrieval:** 96 paired synthetic image/text vectors, cosine matrices, bidirectional Recall@k, true-pair versus maximum-distractor margins, per-query reciprocal-rank changes and group-level paired Recall@1 intervals. Preserve candidate sets and correct pair IDs. Intervals resample matched query identities within predefined groups; they do not include training uncertainty. KDEs are descriptive.
- **Evidence conflict:** synthetic case logits for three conditions. Alluvial counts come from argmax response identity and conserve adjacent marginals. Fixed answer colors apply across stages. The response surface evaluates the same formula with case noise set to zero; it is not an empirical uncertainty surface. Error/coverage uses the stipulated visual target and descending maximum softmax probability; no calibration claim follows. Within-quality-bin effects use paired case bootstrap intervals.
- **Diffusion numerics:** actual numerical integration of the variance-exploding probability-flow ODE on a known 2D eight-mode mixture. An analytic score replaces a learned denoiser. Euler, Heun and midpoint share initial particles and equal score-call budgets; two-stage methods take half as many steps as calls. Error is endpoint L2 distance against a 2,048-call Heun reference at finite sigma .01, not FID, perceptual quality or error against a paired image. Ribbons show particle IQR. Checkpoint plots zoom independently with explicit limits; colors retain final-mode identity. The trajectory panel clips its central viewport, with full paths exported. Nearest-mode distance is a geometric diagnostic, not distributional fidelity.

For real MLLM or image diffusion experiments, replace the generation blocks with actual embeddings, logits, predictions or sampler outputs and recompute every dependent panel. Do not relabel these fixtures as measured model results. Keep group definitions, rank denominators, paired IDs, score-call accounting and the meaning of error explicit.

Composition source studies: [EyeCLIP, npj Digital Medicine (2025), Fig. 5](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01772-2) and [Optical generative models, Nature (2025), Figs. 2–3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09446-5). Their original figures were inspected as layout references; no images, scores or optical-device claims are reproduced. The conflict plate is an original evaluation template.
